03 · The mechanism

We build the cohort around your real workflow.

Generic AI courses teach generic prompts to a generic room. We do the opposite: we measure your actual work for two weeks, then design the weekend around your real bottlenecks. That's why it costs more than a YouTube tutorial, and why it ships.

05 · What you'll build

Real AI automations against real bottlenecks.

These are the kinds of automations past LaunchBrick clients have shipped in production. Your cohort weekend won't build exactly these, yours will be designed from your tracking data. But this is the shape of what comes home with you.

An inbox triage agent that drafts replies in your voice.

Email · Drafting

Reads incoming email, categorizes by intent, and pre-drafts your response based on how you replied to similar emails in the past. You read, edit, send. ~5 hours/week back for most founders.

Gmail→Classifier→Draft in your voice

A meeting-recording → action-items pipeline.

Meetings · Ops

Your Zoom/Fathom/Granola recordings get transcribed, summarized, and turned into structured tasks in Linear, Notion, or ClickUp, assigned to the right person, with deadlines pulled from the conversation.

Recording→Action extraction→Project tool

A proposal drafter trained on your past wins.

Sales · Drafting

Feed it a discovery-call transcript and a one-line brief. It returns a first-draft proposal in your house voice, pulling structure and pricing logic from the proposals you've actually closed.

Discovery notes→Voice-matched draft→Docs / PDF

A CRM enrichment + warm-intro finder.

CRM · Pipeline

Every new contact in your CRM gets auto-enriched: company, role, recent posts, mutual connections. Flags people you should re-engage and drafts the reach-out. Quiet, on a schedule, while you sleep.

06 · The weekend in Bali

Two days. Designed to ship, not to teach.

Every block is framed by what gets built by the end of it. You will not sit in front of slides. We measure success by what's running in your accounts on the way home to the villa.

Saturday

Day 01 · Build
09:00

Theory block: the AI toolbox, in plain English.

A 90-minute tour of what's actually out there: RAG, agents, Skills, MCPs, tools, vector search, enrichment. You leave with a working mental map of what each thing is for, so you can spot the right tool for the right workflow.

10:30

Personal briefing: your workflow report.

Walk through your 14-day tracking output. Confirm the two or three automations we'll ship for you this weekend.

11:00

Build block 01: your first automation.

Scaffolding, account access, first deploy by lunch. By noon, something runs end-to-end against your real data.

13:00

Lunch on site · cohort working session.

Founders compare notes on what they're shipping. Dani rotates between builds, unblocking.

14:30

Build block 02: refine + handle edge cases.

The boring 80% that makes an agent actually usable in production: error handling, voice tuning, retry logic.

17:30

Demo round 01: ship what you have.

Each founder walks the cohort through what's now running. We commit Saturday's work to your own repos.

Sunday

Day 02 · Deploy
09:00

Theory block: workflow-spotting framework.

How to recognise an automatable workflow in your business: triggers, repeatable steps, decision points, integration surfaces. The same lens we used to design your Saturday builds, so you can apply it solo next month.

10:30

Build block 03: your second automation.

Start your second build with everything you learned Saturday. Most founders move ~3x faster on day two.

12:30

Lunch + workflow-spotting exercise.

We map the next five automations you could ship in your business. Goes home with you as a 90-day plan.

14:00

Build block 04: third automation or polish.

Depending on scope: ship a third lightweight agent, or harden the first two for daily use by your team.

17:00

Production deploys + handover docs.

Final deploy to your accounts. You leave with READMEs, credentials, and a runbook for each automation.

19:00

Closing dinner.

Cohort dinner. Slack channel opens. The 60-day clock starts.

11 · Honest questions

The things you're actually thinking.

I'm not technical. Will I actually be able to build something?
+
Yes — by design. You'll work in natural-language interfaces, paired one-on-one with Dani, who has spent 7 years teaching non-coders to ship. The cohort is capped at 4–5 so nobody gets left behind.
What's the workflow-tracking software? Is it a keylogger?
+
No. You pick from a vetted list (Rize, Timing, Toggl Track in auto-mode). They measure which app you're in and for how long, never keystrokes or screen content. Pause any time. You own the data; we see only aggregated reports.
What happens to my data and credentials?
+
Everything is deployed to your own accounts, on your own infrastructure. API keys live in your password manager, not ours. We never store your customer data. After the cohort, you can rotate every credential we touched in under 5 minutes, and we'll show you exactly how on Sunday afternoon.
What's the refund policy?
+
If we're not a fit, you'll know on the intake call and won't be charged. Full refund up to 14 days before the cohort. If by lunch on Saturday you feel this isn't what you expected, we refund 100% on the spot.
Where exactly is it in Bali? Do I need to arrange anything?
+
The venue is Canggu, Bali. It's a private working space with reliable fiber, indoor and outdoor zones, and a quiet kitchen. You arrange your own flights and accommodation, we'll send a list of three vetted villas within walking distance, at three price points. Lunches and both dinners are included.
What do I literally walk out with on Sunday?
+
A folder on your laptop with: (a) source code or workflow definitions for 2–3 automations, (b) the credentials and accounts they run on, (c) a one-page runbook per automation, (d) a 90-day plan for the next five automations you could ship, and (e) a Slack invite to the 60-day cohort channel.
My business is unusual, agency / fund / e-com / regulated. Will this still work?
+
Probably. The mechanism doesn't care about industry, it cares whether you have a repeatable workflow with measurable friction. We'll know in 15 minutes on the intake call.
What timezone is the 60-day Slack support in?
+
Dani works primarily on Bali time (GMT+8) and is reachable on a rolling business-hours basis that covers Asia-Pacific mornings and EU mornings. Most threads get a response within 24 hours; urgent items within a few. The cohort Slack is async-first, so you can also lean on your cohort-mates across time zones.
What if my automations break a week after the cohort?
+
That's what the 60-day support is for. Send a message, we diagnose, you patch. You'll also leave with a "what to check first" runbook per agent, most breakages get fixed before you message.
Why so small, only 4–5 founders?
+
Because we promise pair-built automations with the instructor. That math only works at four or five. A bigger room means slides, watered-down attention, and "I'll get to you next", which is exactly the model the Academy is designed against. Small is the product.
