07 — Questions

The things you're probably thinking.

How fast can you really deliver custom business software?
+
Most projects ship a working v1 in 4–8 weeks. Our edge isn't magic — it's a focused stack we've shipped 50+ times, an AI-assisted build pipeline, and a refusal to spend three months on slide decks. We'll tell you on the discovery call exactly what we can hit.
What about data privacy and private AI agents?
+
Everything runs on private infrastructure under your business's name. Your customer data is never sent to OpenAI, Anthropic, or any third-party LLM you didn't explicitly approve. Private AI agents run locally on the same servers as your app — auditable, replaceable, and yours.
Can you handle my specific industry?
+
We've built for ecommerce, agencies, professional services, marketplaces, fintech wallets, and operations-heavy SMEs. Custom software development is industry-agnostic by definition — the question is whether your workflow is well enough understood to encode it. That's what discovery is for.
Do I actually own the code?
+
Yes — fully. Source code, database, deployment keys, infrastructure access. From the day we ship, you can walk away, hire another team, or run it yourself. No usage fees, no per-seat charges, no lock-in. Spelt out in plain English in our contract.
What does it cost?
+
Project-based, scoped after the discovery call. Most v1 builds land between $15k and $60k depending on scope, with optional ongoing support. We'll give you a fixed quote before you commit — no time-and-materials surprises.
What if I just need part of my SaaS stack rebuilt?
+
That's how most projects start. Pick the workflow that hurts most — payroll, support, ordering — we replace that first. Once it's live and you trust the process, we keep going and replace the next subscription.
