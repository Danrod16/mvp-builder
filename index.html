01 · The problem

Everyone pays for AI. Almost nobody has it working.

Your team has ChatGPT tabs, a Claude seat, maybe a Copilot license. Everyone uses them differently, nothing is connected, and every conversation starts from zero.

01
Every chat starts from scratch. The AI never remembers last week's meeting, the client email, or what you decided on Tuesday.
02
Nobody checks the output. One confident wrong answer and the team stops trusting the tool.
03
One person in the company gets AI. Everyone else copies prompts from LinkedIn.
04
Pilots never leave the pilot. You bought the tools, watched the demos, and nothing changed in how the work gets done.
05
Company data gets pasted into whatever tool is open, with no idea where it ends up.
02 · What we install

Four parts. One system that does the work.

This is the setup from the diagram above. That client runs a product team, but the same pattern works for support, ops, sales, and content. We adapt the rules, the bones stay the same.

01 · Lead agent

One agent you talk to

You give it a goal in plain English, from your phone or laptop. It breaks the work down, picks the right model for each task, and hands it out.

  • Brief it from anywhere
  • Best model per task
  • Queues up to 10 tasks
02 · Workers

A team that runs in parallel

Each worker gets one task and its own isolated workspace, so nobody steps on anybody's work. They keep going while you do something else.

  • Own worktree each
  • Parallel by default
  • Report back when done
03 · Guardrails

Nothing ships unchecked

The lead agent reviews every result before you see it. It scans for security holes, turns every user story into a test, and runs every change through CI.

  • Every output verified
  • Security scans
  • Tests and CI on every PR
04 · Memory

It remembers everything

Every session, email, and meeting transcript gets saved automatically. Your AI knows what was decided last month, and so does the next person you hire.

  • Claude sessions
  • Email
  • Meeting notes (Granola)
03 · How it works

From first call to a system your team runs.

Hands-on from day one. We build it with you, in your accounts, so nothing leaves when we do.

Step 01 · Audit

We map where the hours go.

We sit with you and the people doing the work. We find the workflows where AI saves real time, and the ones where it shouldn't touch anything.

Step 02 · Setup

We install the system.

Hardware, subscriptions, lead agent, workers, memory, hooks. All in your accounts, on a machine you own, with rules written for how your company works.

Step 03 · Training

Your team learns to run it.

We work next to your team on real tasks until they can brief the lead agent, review its work, and fix it when it's wrong.

Ongoing · Advisory

We stay on call.

Models change every few months. We keep your setup current, add new workflows, and step in when something breaks.

07 · Questions

The things you're probably thinking.

Do I need to be technical?
+
No. You need to know your business. We handle the setup and train you to brief the lead agent in plain English. If you can write a clear brief for a new hire, you can run this.
What hardware and subscriptions do I need?
+
Usually one Mac mini and an AI subscription in your company's name, like Claude. We'll tell you exactly what to buy on the first call. You never pay us a software license.
Is my data safe?
+
The system runs on a machine you own, under accounts you control. We set up who can access what, and the lead agent scans every change for vulnerabilities before it ships. Nothing sits on our servers.
We're not a software company. Is this for us?
+
Yes. The example on this page runs a product team, but the same pattern works for support, ops, sales, and content. A lead agent, workers, guardrails, and memory fit any team with repeatable work.
What does it cost?
+
A fixed price for the audit and setup, scoped after the first call. Advisory after that is optional and monthly. You'll know the full number before you commit.
Do you still build custom software?
+
Yes. If the audit shows you need a custom app, we can build it. Most clients find they need less software once their AI is set up properly.
